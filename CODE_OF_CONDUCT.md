# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We expect everyone taking part in this project, in any role, to treat others
with respect. Harassment of any kind is not acceptable, and neither is
dismissiveness, bad faith, or deliberate obstruction of other people's work.

This project is a catalogue of attacks on AI systems. Its subject matter is
hostile by definition, and it is easy to import the tone of the subject into the
discussion. Descriptions of attacks, exploits, and harm belong in entry files,
not in how people speak to each other.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps the project:

- Being accurate about what a source does and does not say, and correcting
  yourself when a source is read more carefully.
- Arguing with the evidence rather than with the person presenting it. A
  re-tiered entry is a discussion about tiering.
- Accepting that a conservative grade is a feature, not an insult.
- Giving credit, keeping the tone plain, and assuming good faith.

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, in public or in private.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Threatening, coercing, or intimidating anyone, including over a disagreement
  about an entry's tier or class.
- Impersonating another contributor or a maintainer.
- Sustained disruption of issues, pull requests, or discussions.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably make someone unwilling to
  contribute.

## Our responsibilities

The maintainers of this project are responsible for:

- Clarifying these standards where it is unclear what is expected.
- Taking fair, appropriate, and proportionate corrective action in response to
  behaviour they consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.
- Removing, editing, or rejecting contributions that do not meet these
  standards, including permanently banning a contributor from further
  participation.

Maintainer decisions are made on the evidence available. Decisions that
affect a contributor's ability to participate are reversible on new evidence
and can be appealed as described below.

## Our scope

These standards apply in all project spaces: the issue tracker, pull requests
and reviews, discussions, and any other space run by the project. They also
apply when an individual is officially representing the project in public,
including when they speak for it, post from an official account, or act as an
appointed representative in another space.

Use of a project space, or of a project mailing list, is a form of
representation. Contributors submitting an entry are representing the
project's standards whether or not they are a maintainer.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour should be
reported through the repository's **Security** tab on GitHub, using the private
report channel available there. Do not open a public issue to report a
violation, and do not post the details of an active enforcement case in a
public space. No personal email address is published for reports; the Security
tab is the reporting channel for this project.

Reports go to the maintainer responsible for enforcement, who is the maintainer
of this repository.

The maintainer will:

1. Review the report, including the account, context, and behaviour described.
2. Acknowledge the reporter and, where a violation is found, explain the
   decision.
3. Determine consequences appropriate to the violation. Options include a
   private correction, a public warning, a temporary suspension from
   participation, or a permanent ban. Severity, intent, recurrence, and impact on
   other contributors are the factors.
4. Keep the identity and the details of the reporter confidential to the extent
   possible.

Not reporting a violation you witness, when it is safe and reasonable to do so,
is itself a violation.

The maintainer may consult other contributors to understand a report better.
Where more than one maintainer is involved in a case, none of them decides it
alone.

## Appeals

If you disagree with an enforcement decision, reply through the same private
report channel with what you believe was missed. A different maintainer reviews
the appeal where one is available. Appeals are considered on the record as it
stood, not on new public discussion of the person involved.
